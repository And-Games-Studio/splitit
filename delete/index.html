Split It!

Delete your data

You can have your Split It! account and its data deleted at any time, even without the game installed.

How to ask

Send an email to ebano.andrea.games@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your friend code, which you'll find in the game under Friends (for example "BlueFox42#12345"). If you no longer have the game, tell us your nickname and, if you used it, the Google Play Games name you played with.

We delete the data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your Unity player account: friend code and chosen name, friends, friend requests, blocked players, and your progress saved in the cloud.
  • The link between that account and your Google Play Games profile.
  • Your anonymous Epic Online Services account used for online play, which we ask Epic to delete.
  • Any report you sent us, or that was sent about you.

What stays

  • Your Google Play Games profile, which belongs to Google: you manage it in the Google Play Games app.
  • Data on your phone: uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android's settings, removes it.

Deleted data can't be recovered: friends you had will need to add you again. Details are in our privacy policy.
