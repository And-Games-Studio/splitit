Split It!

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

In short: Split It! has no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking or analytics. It doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. Playing alone, your progress stays on your phone. Online features (playing with others, friends, saving your progress to your Google account) need a few pieces of data, described below, which are handled by Epic Games, Unity and Google on our behalf.

Who we are

Split It! is made by Andrea Ebano, publishing as and.games, based in Portugal. Andrea Ebano is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: ebano.andrea.games@gmail.com.

What data the game uses, and why

Playing alone

Your progress (levels, best scores), settings, generated nickname and chosen character are stored on your phone only, unless you are signed in to Google Play Games (see "Your progress in the cloud").

Playing online (Epic Online Services)

When you open Online, the game signs in to Epic Online Services, provided by Epic Games, with an anonymous account tied to a random identifier created on your phone. You don't need an Epic account and we don't ask who you are. Epic receives:

  • that anonymous identifier, and your generated nickname (for example "BlueFox42");
  • the rooms you create or join, and what other players in your room see: your nickname, your character, and your friends identifier (so they can add you as a friend);
  • the game data that travels between the players of a match (moves, shots, scores).

During a match, players' phones connect to each other, directly or through Epic's relay servers. In a direct connection the other players' devices can see your IP address, as in any peer-to-peer game.

Friends (Unity Gaming Services)

Friends use Unity Gaming Services, provided by Unity Technologies. When you open Friends, play online, or are signed in to Google Play Games, the game signs you in to an anonymous Unity player account. Unity stores:

  • your player identifier and your friend code: your nickname, or a name you chose, followed by a number;
  • your friends, friend requests and the players you blocked;
  • while the game is open, your status as your friends see it: online or offline, whether you're in the menus, playing alone, in a room or in a match, and the code of your room when they can join it;
  • invites you send to friends.

A name you choose is shown only to your friends; other players see your generated nickname. Names are checked against a list of offensive words before they're accepted. The last players you played with online are kept on your phone, so you can add them as friends later.

Google Play Games (Android)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game uses it to sign in to your Unity player account and links the two, so you get your progress and friends back on a new phone. The game receives your Play Games player identifier and display name, and Google gives Unity a one-time code to confirm your account. Google Play Games is a Google service, covered by Google's privacy policy.

Your progress in the cloud (Unity Cloud Save)

When you are signed in with Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is kept with your Unity player account: best scores, the Classic game to continue, your nickname and chosen name, whether you accepted the name rules, your character, and whether you use Friends.

Reports

When you report a player, your email app opens with a message to us containing the reported player's name and identifier and your own friend code and identifier. Nothing is sent unless you send that email. We use reports only to deal with them, and keep them as long as that takes, at most 12 months.

Sharing an invite

"Share" passes a short text with your room code to the app you pick. We don't see whom you send it to.

Technical data

Like any online service, Epic, Unity and Google receive your IP address and basic technical information when the game connects to them, to make the connection work and keep the services secure. The Unity engine may also send Unity anonymous information about your device (model, operating system, graphics hardware) when the game starts.

Legal bases

We use this data to provide the features you choose to use (Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR), and, for reports, blocking and service security, because of our legitimate interest in keeping the game safe (Article 6(1)(f)).

How long we keep it

Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data kept by Unity and Epic for your accounts stays while you use them, and is deleted when you ask us to. Reports are kept at most 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. Write to ebano.andrea.games@gmail.com. To delete your data, see Delete your data. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Portugal, the CNPD (cnpd.pt), or the one where you live.

Children

Split It! is meant for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you think a child has used the online features, write to us and we'll delete the data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
