Split It!

Shoot. Split. Survive.

Walk, fire your harpoon and split every bubble until none is left. Big bubbles split into smaller ones, and the smallest pop for good.

Play the levels alone, or online with up to four players: together in one arena, or against each other in arenas of your own, sending bubbles over to the others.
